# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

Participants are expected to be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing private information are not acceptable.

## Expected behavior

- Focus criticism on technical work, not people.
- Explain disagreements with evidence and respect differing experience levels.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them constructively.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove unacceptable contributions and may restrict participation when necessary. Report concerns privately through the repository's available contact or reporting mechanisms.
